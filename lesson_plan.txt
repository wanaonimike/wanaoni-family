Strand: The Early Life of Jesus Christ
Sub-strand: The Birth of Jesus Christ

Specific Learning Outcomes:
1. Identify the parent of Jesus Christ.
2. Name the parents of Jesus Christ.
3. Show desire to celebrate the birth of Jesus by singing a Christian carol song.

Key Inquiry Questions:
- Why do we celebrate the birth of Jesus Christ?
- What was the state of the place where Jesus Christ was born?

Organization of Learning:
Method: Work in pairs
Core Competence: Communication and collaboration
Values: Love and respect
Learning Resources: Digital devices and Bible

Lesson Flow (30 Minutes):
1. Introduction (5 minutes):
   Sing a song related to the birth of Jesus to engage learners and set a joyful tone.

2. Development – Step 1 (10 minutes):
   Read Luke 1:26–38 together and discuss who Jesus’ parents are and where He was born.

3. Development – Step 2 (8 minutes):
   Watch a short video clip on Jesus’ birth. In pairs, learners discuss what they saw and answer the inquiry questions.

4. Activity (5 minutes):
   As a class, sing a chosen Christmas carol to celebrate the birth of Jesus, reinforcing joy and community spirit.

5. Extended Activity (Homework):
   Draw and colour a manger scene at home. Learners will bring their artwork to share during the next lesson.

Reflection (Maria Montessori Approach):
1. Foster a supportive environment where each child feels valued and safe to participate.
2. Encourage peer support by pairing learners with different abilities so that stronger readers assist emerging readers.
3. Allow extra time and freedom of movement for learners with attention challenges (e.g., let them stand or move to a quieter spot).
4. Use visual and non-verbal aids such as picture cards of Mary, Joseph, and the manger to reinforce understanding.
5. Provide concrete materials like a miniature stable model and textured nativity figures to engage sight, touch, and hearing.
