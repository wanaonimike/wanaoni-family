# Christian Religious Education – 42 Questions

1. After Adam and Eve sinned and discovered their nakedness, God made clothes from animal skin for them.  
   What attribute of God is shown here?  
   A. Sole creator  B. Loving  C. Organised  D. All powerful

2. Kate lives with her father and two brothers.  
   What kind of family does she come from?  
   A. Nuclear family  B. Extended family  C. Single parent family  D. Non-existent family

3. Which pair consists of Old Testament books only?  
   A. Leviticus and Malachi  B. Acts and Joel  C. Ruth and Naomi  D. Romans and Philippians

4. The book of Luke 19:2–10 tells the story of Zacchaeus.  
   What lesson do we learn from this story?  
   A. Zacchaeus was a very good man  B. Jesus came to save sinners  
   C. It is okay to steal to get rich  D. Jesus hates short people

5. From Numbers 22:1–6, 20–34, a man beats his donkey until it speaks.  
   Who is this man?  
   A. Balaam  B. Saul  C. Balaak  D. Samuel

6. Jesus used parables to teach.  
   Which parable did He tell about the growth of God’s kingdom?  
   A. Mustard seed  B. Prodigal son  C. Lost coin  D. Healing Blind Bartimaeus

7. Which value can help develop talents and abilities?  
   A. Assertiveness  B. Critical thinking  C. Hard work  D. Creative thinking

8. A talent is a natural gift.  
   Who did God give skill and ability for artistic work?  
   A. Solomon  B. Bezalel  C. Oholiab  D. David

9. During a class discussion, you're tasked to present the Ten Commandments.  
   Which commandment teaches us to speak the truth?  
   A. Do not kill  B. Do not accuse falsely  C. Thou shall not covet  D. Honour your father and mother

10. A woman who had been bleeding for twelve years was healed by touching Jesus’ cloth.  
    What made her well?  
    A. Doctor’s medication  B. Her honesty  C. Her faith  D. Jesus rubbed mud in her eyes

11. Christians endure suffering for Christ’s sake.  
    Which virtue did Apostle Paul practice?  
    A. Perseverance  B. Anger  C. Impatience  D. Dishonesty

12. Which temptation did Jesus face in the desert?  
    A. Throw Himself from the pinnacle  B. Change water into wine  
    C. Stop His mission  D. Deny His disciples

13. Which group represents the Holy Trinity?  
    A. Father, mother, and children  B. God the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit  
    C. Jesus, Mary, and Joseph  D. God the Father, Moses, and Elijah

14. Who was among the first four disciples called by Jesus?  
    A. Andrew  B. Judas Iscariot  C. Bartholomew  D. Matthew

15. Jesus performed many miracles.  
    Which miracle shows He is the universal saviour?  
    A. Calming the storm  B. Healing Blind Bartimaeus  
    C. Healing the Roman officer’s servant  D. Raising Lazarus

16. Genesis 12:2 — "I will bless who bless you, but I will curse those who curse you..."  
    These words were said by God to:  
    A. Noah  B. David  C. Adam  D. Abraham

17. Jacob wrestled with an Angel of God at Peniel until daybreak.  
    What lesson do we learn from this story?  
    A. Being courageous in life  B. Being determined in life  
    C. Being armed to fight our enemies  D. Being humble in prayer

18. Christians are reminded of the need to help the needy.  
    Which parable did Jesus teach about this?  
    A. The rich man and Lazarus  B. The bleeding woman  
    C. The friend at midnight  D. The unforgiving servant

19. Learners discussed the temptations of Jesus in Luke 4:1–13.  
    Which group correctly ordered them?  
    A. Group A (iii)(ii)(i)  B. Group B (ii)(i)(iii)  
    C. Group M (i)(iii)  D. Group T (iii)(ii)(i)

20. What is a similarity between the birth and life of Jesus and John the Baptist?  
    A. Both were baptized by one person  B. Both births were announced by an angel  
    C. Both parents were Joseph and Mary  D. Both taught on repentance

21. Which cluster comprises only Old Testament books?  
    A. Acts, Romans, Nahum, Micah  B. Exodus, Titus, Joel, James  
    C. Job, Judges, Ezra, Exodus  D. Luke, Lamentations, Numbers, Jude

22. After reading Matthew 8:5–13 about the healing of the Roman officer’s servant, we learn all of the following *except*:  
    A. Employers should take care of their workers  B. Christians should neglect the sick  
    C. We should have faith in God  D. We should seek help from God

23. Which one of the following values can be used to measure honesty?  
    A. Respect  B. Integrity  C. Humility  D. Kindness

24. Who among the following recovered an axe head from the river?  
    A. Elisha  B. Elijah  C. Samuel  D. Nathan

25. What was the name of the son born to Sarah when she was ninety years old?  
    A. Isaac  B. Ishmael  C. Jacob  D. Joseph

26. Who among the following was sold to the Ishmaelites by his brothers?  
    A. Joseph  B. Benjamin  C. Reuben  D. Simeon

27. Who among the following was the first king of Israel?  
    A. Saul  B. David  C. Solomon  D. Rehoboam

28. Who among the following was a tax collector before he became a disciple of Jesus?  
    A. Matthew  B. Peter  C. John  D. Andrew

29. Who among the following was the mother of John the Baptist?  
    A. Elizabeth  B. Mary  C. Martha  D. Anna

30. Who among the following was the first Christian martyr?  
    A. Stephen  B. James  C. Peter  D. Paul

31. Who among the following was the Roman governor who sentenced Jesus to be crucified?  
    A. Pontius Pilate  B. Herod  C. Caesar  D. Felix

32. Why is the Bible an inspired word of God?  
    A. It teaches about God  B. It was written by holy men  
    C. It is used in churches  D. It is free from error

33. Jesus was baptized in River Jordan.  
    A. John  B. Peter  C. Jesus  D. Thomas

34. The king of Israel that had many wives was  
    A. Solomon  B. Saul  C. David  D. Jonathan

35. The first book in the New Testament is  
    A. Genesis  B. Acts  C. Matthew  D. Revelation

36. Which is not a good practice for a Christian?  
    A. Keeping the Sabbath day Holy  B. Helping the needy  
    C. Praising God  D. Going to church

37. Which of the following was created on the third day of creation?  
    A. Sea and birds  B. Land and vegetation  
    C. Sky and stars  D. Man

38. Christians ought to forgive one another because  
    A. It is a commandment  B. God forgave our sins  
    C. It is good to forgive  D. It is a promise

39. Which of the following is not a fruit of the Holy Spirit?  
    A. Humility  B. Kindness  C. Self-control  D. Patience

40. The following are lessons learnt from the healing of the centurion’s servant *except*:  
    A. Faith  B. Good health  C. God’s help  D. Care for others

41. Which of the following qualities was Jesus praised for by the centurion?  
    A. Love  B. Kindness  C. Power  D. Authority

42. When did Jesus go to the wilderness to be tempted by Satan?  
    A. After baptism  B. After the sermon on the mount  
    C. After the Last Supper  D. After the transfiguration
